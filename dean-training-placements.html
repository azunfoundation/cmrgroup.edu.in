Meet Dr. P. Hima Bindu

Dr. P. Hima Bindu serves as the Dean – Training & Placements at CMR Group of Institutions, bringing over 18 years of rich experience spanning corporate, academic, and research domains. She holds an MBA, B.Ed., M.Phil., and a Ph.D. in Human Resource Management from Osmania University.

With a strong foundation in HRM and student development, Dr. Hima Bindu has contributed extensively to research, with multiple publications and conference presentations to her credit. She has further strengthened her expertise through a certification in HR Analytics from IIM Rohtak.

In her leadership role, she plays a pivotal part in shaping industry-ready graduates by designing and implementing structured training programs that focus on both technical proficiency and soft skills. Her approach emphasizes holistic student development, combining academic knowledge with practical exposure, mentorship, and career guidance.

Known for her strong mentorship abilities, she actively engages with students to enhance their employability, guiding them through skill development, interview preparation, and career planning. Her efforts have consistently contributed to strengthening placement outcomes and building meaningful industry connections for the institution.

"My vision is to ensure every student at CMR Group is not just placed, but placed right — in a role that matches their aspirations, skills, and potential for long-term professional growth."
